At Antique Designs, everything is authentic and physically handcrafted. Established over 13 years ago on Magadi Main Road, Bengaluru, our atelier specialises in traditional sheet metal embossing (Repoussé & Chasing), sand casting, laser lattice cutwork, and chemical metal patination.

Every main entrance door, pooja mandir entrance, staircase balustrade, and designer handle is physically shaped by hands with decades of generational craft experience. We collaborate directly with homeowners, luxury interior architects, and temple trusts across South India.

Generations of Pure Metal Mastery

How We Handcraft Your Artifact

Every commissioned piece undergoes rigorous traditional metallurgic stages in our Bengaluru foundry.

01

Architectural Drafting

We review site dimensions, architectural elevations, and Vastu requirements to design 1:1 scale custom templates and approval drawings.

02

Manual Hand Embossing

Master artisans chase and hammer virgin brass sheets over pitch blocks to raise intricate, deep-relief sculptures by hand — no machines.

03

Solid Wood Cladding

Embossed metal panels are mechanically anchored to moisture-treated, seasoned teak wood frames with brass rivets and countersunk bolts.

04

Polish & Clear Coat

Hand-buffed with jeweller's rouge and sealed with dual-component anti-oxidant lacquer for lifelong shine without maintenance.

Available Alloys & Finishes

Pure Metals & Protective Coatings

Every piece is crafted from certified metals, sealed with specialized anti-oxidant lacquer, available in four exclusive finishes.

Pure Gold Brass

Traditional golden lustre reflecting timeless luxury and auspicious Indian temple aesthetics. Most popular for main doors.

White Metal (Silver)

Bright silver finish popular for pooja mandirs, temple sanctums, and contemporary minimalist luxury spaces.

Antique Bronze Patina

Hand-rubbed dark rustic patina creating deep shadow relief on carved and embossed panels — timeless heritage look.

Rich Copper / Rose Gold

Warm coppery tones offering an ultra-luxurious, majestic presence for statement entryways and accent pieces.
