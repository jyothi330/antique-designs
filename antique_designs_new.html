OUR HERITAGE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Preserving the Soul of Centuries-Old Treasures

Founded with a deep reverence for classical antiquity, Antique Designs travels across royal estates, European châteaux, and historic auction houses to uncover one-of-a-kind art pieces and architectural salvage.

Every creation in our vault goes through a comprehensive authentication protocol — including dendrochronology wood testing, metallurgical analysis, and archival provenance checks before it is offered to our private collectors.

Complete Provenance Dossier: Backed by museum curators and historical documentation.

Bespoke Artisan Restoration: French polishing, marquetry repair, and authentic horology.

White-Glove International Delivery: Climate-controlled custom crating with full insurance.
