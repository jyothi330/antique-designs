# Antique Designs — Official Website Replacement Guide

## About the Business
**Antique Designs** is an authentic, offline artisan manufacturing atelier and brass/white metal foundry based on Magadi Main Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka (Established 2014).
They handcraft, emboss, cast, and forge real architectural brass and metal pieces for villas, luxury residences, and temples.

---

## 9 Core Architectural Product Lines:
1. **Pooja Room & Mandir Doors** (Gopuram arches, kalasha, bell cutouts)
2. **Grand Brass Main Entrance Doors** (16-gauge embossed brass cladding, lion knockers, studs)
3. **Symmetrical Double Doors** (Regal two-leaf doors with mirror relief work)
4. **3/4 Asymmetrical Concept Doors** (Modern asymmetric luxury entryways)
5. **Forged Brass Railings & Balusters** (Staircase helical handrails and acanthus balusters)
6. **Bespoke Cast Brass Name Plates** (Sand-cast letters, family crests, teak/marble backing)
7. **Luxury Partition Jaali Grills** (Laser-cut & cast brass room dividers)
8. **Grand Compound & Villa Gates** (Heavy-duty iron frames with ornate brass inserts)
9. **High-Relief 3D Metal Wall Murals** (Hand-hammered repoussé Tree of Life, peacock, lotus)
