Polishing, patination and surface finishing.

Surfaces receive manual jeweller's rouge buffing, chemical patination baths, antique shading, or high-gloss satin polishing. Each piece is hermetically sealed with dual-component anti-oxidant coatings to permanently lock in lustre and protect against environmental tarnish.

  • Hand-Rubbed Dark Patina
